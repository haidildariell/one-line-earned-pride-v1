Entry for the [One Line of Earned Pride](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557) bounty on make.fun.

The program (`main.ts`, Node 22) writes a single line of text — 1 to 240 UTF-8
characters — to `/output/entry.txt`: a quiet, earned moment of pride for
someone in a scene. A boy who soldered a broken radio back together seventeen
times finally hears his grandmother's favourite song come through the static
clear — and just sits there, grinning. No trophy, no podium, no speech: the
effort is inside the line, and the pride lands on the last word.
